## Business Questions
1. Which companies laid off the most employees?
2. Which industries were most affected by layoffs?
3. Which countries experienced the highest layoffs?
4. How did layoffs change over time (yearly and monthly)?
5. Is there a relationship between funds raised and layoffs?

## Key Analysis
- Layoffs by company
- Layoffs by industry
- Layoffs by country
- Monthly layoffs trend
- Funds raised vs layoffs correlation

## Key Insights
- The United States recorded the highest layoffs globally.
- Retail and Hardware industries experienced the largest layoffs.
- Layoffs peaked during 2023.
- Major companies such as Amazon and Intel had the highest layoffs.
- The correlation between funds raised and layoffs is weak (around 0.11).
- This suggests that higher funding does not necessarily prevent layoffs.
- Layoffs are influenced by broader factors such as market conditions and company strategy.
